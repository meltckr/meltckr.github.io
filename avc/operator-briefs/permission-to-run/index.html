Worked hypothetical · Every event and figure is invented

Friday, 7:10 p.m.

F&I receives a worksheet showing $18,000 for the customer's trade. The desk appraised it at $17,000. The salesperson has already quoted $18,000. Under this example's store rule, the desk manager can add $500 to the appraisal. The remaining $500 needs the GM's approval.

The GM is off-site. The desk sends a message: "Need you on a trade." It leaves out the figures. Approval arrives at 7:28. The desk corrects the worksheet, and F&I can resume at 7:34. The deal has waited 24 minutes.

Scenario calculation

What the hold costs in this example

Assume every other deal figure stays fixed and the extra trade allowance comes out of expected gross profit. The $1,000 exception uses $1,000 of that gross. The delay also takes 24 minutes from the buyer and the staff. This example assigns no payroll cost or lost-sale value to those minutes.

Suggested store rule

Who approves what on Monday

Have the GM sign a short approval rule. In this example, the desk can approve up to $17,500 on a $17,000 appraisal. The GM or assigned backup approves anything higher. Your store should set its own dollar limits using its deal economics.

Put the backup's name and contact route beside the rule. If the trial reply deadline passes, the desk calls that person. Keep the request and the answer with the worksheet so the next person can see what was approved.

Salesperson

Quote the approved trade figure. If a different figure has already been promised, tell the desk exactly what the customer heard.

Desk manager

Check the appraisal against the promised figure. Send the over-limit request to the named approver. Stay with the deal until F&I confirms receipt of the approved worksheet.

GM or assigned backup

Approve the amount or return a figure the desk can take to the customer. Put the answer in writing. Name the backup before leaving the store.

F&I manager

Read back the approved trade figure when accepting the worksheet. Flag any mismatch before preparing the customer's final paperwork.

Suggested script · Same hypothetical deal

Send one message the GM can answer

At 7:10, the desk manager could send this:

Trade appraisal: $17,000. Customer was quoted $18,000. My approval limit is $17,500.

Please approve $18,000 or return a figure I can take back to the customer. F&I has paused the worksheet. Please answer by 7:15. I'm staying with this deal.

A suggested GM reply

Approved at $18,000. I accept the $1,000 difference. Send F&I the revised worksheet with this approval.

The desk sends both items together. F&I replies with the trade figure it received. The salesperson gives the customer the next update.

Complete handoff

Aim for every over-limit worksheet to carry its approval.

Record the share sent with written approval. Count returned worksheets once per deal; note each missing or conflicting trade figure.

Monday review

Read the slowest request on Monday

Give the desk manager the week's log before opening. Read the slowest request with the person who had to answer it. If that person had no named backup, put the backup on next week's schedule.
